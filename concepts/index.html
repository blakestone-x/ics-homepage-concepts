Three directions for the homepage.

Each is a working prototype, not a slide. Click into any one and browse it as a visitor would — every page links to the other two from the bar at the top, so you can compare them without coming back here.

The operating brief

A consulting register: numbered sections and exhibits, restrained typography, and an interactive coverage lookup that answers “do you cover my sites?” in a few clicks.

Open 02 — Signal

The branded direction

Our own logo, palette and voice, brought up to date. The closest of the three to something we could build from where the current site already is.

Open 03 — Command

The ambitious one

A darker, instrument-led take: a live-feeling coverage map, and a single service call told minute by minute from the first reading to the store coming back up.
